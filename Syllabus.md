## Introduction ##
We have entered a time in which vast amounts of data are more widely available than ever before. At the same time, new tools have been developed to analyze this data and provide decision makers with insights to accomplish their goals. Individuals who engage with data interpretation for organizational leaders often call themselves *data scientists*, and their craft *data science*. Other commonly used terms include **Big Data**, **Predictive Analytics**, and **Data Mining**. This course aims to demystify these concepts for future organizational leaders.

---

## Learning Goals ##
- Understanding data structures and measurement
- Understanding how models can be used to make predictions
- Understanding how results of data analysis are presented
- Understanding how data science powers AI prediction to deepen AI literacy
