## Overview

This module focuses on communicating data science findings clearly and responsibly. Students will learn to tailor their presentations for decision-makers, explore ethical frameworks for data science, and refine their final project deliverables.

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this module, you should be able to:

1. **Communicate** research findings clearly to non-technical audiences
1. **Structure** a data science presentation for organizational decision-makers
1. **Identify** ethical responsibilities in data collection, analysis, and reporting
1. **Apply** ethical frameworks to your group project

## Key Concepts

- Storytelling with data
- Executive summaries and data narratives
- Research ethics and IRB considerations
- Data privacy and informed consent
- Avoiding misrepresentation of findings
