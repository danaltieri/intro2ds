# Introduction to Data Science

**Vanderbilt University Peabody College**  
Leadership and Learning in Organizations Doctoral Program

## Welcome

This course provides a comprehensive introduction to data science methods and tools for doctoral students in Leadership and Learning in Organizations. Over 14 weeks, you’ll develop practical skills in data collection, analysis, and visualization while building a foundation in research methodology.

## Course Overview

This 14-week course covers:

- **Research Design & Methodology** - Designing rigorous research studies
- **Data Collection & Measurement** - Survey design, validity, reliability
- **Statistical Analysis** - Descriptive and inferential statistics
- **Data Visualization** - Creating effective visualizations in Excel
- **Regression & Modeling** - Correlation, regression, and predictive modeling
- **Applied Analytics** - Real-world data science applications

## Course Structure

Each weekly module includes:

- **Required Readings** - From course textbooks and academic articles
- **Recorded Lectures** - Pre-recorded content to watch before class
- **Live Lectures** - Interactive sessions with discussion and examples
- **Exercises** - Hands-on practice with data and tools
- **Assignments** - Applied projects demonstrating mastery
