## Discussed topics

### Critical Multiplism Principles

Professor Daniel led a discussion on the core principles of critical multiplism and how it differs from other research approaches.
- **Details**
    - **Evan**: Noted that Dr. Garcia is critical of single-approach solutions to problems
    - **Catherine**: Emphasized that critical multiplism acknowledges there is no single right way to approach data analysis - "it depends" on the data and goals
    - **Pitts**: Explained that critical multiplism helps eliminate bias in data observation, analysis, and evaluation
    - **E.J.**: Added that numbers aren't sacrosanct and are shaped by potentially biased forces
    - **Kylie**: Suggested the "critical" aspect involves identifying assumptions and biases in different measures
    - **Adrienne**: Mentioned critical multiplism addresses over-reliance on single data sources
    - **Jonathan**: Noted that unlike other methods that try to minimize bias, critical multiplism views bias as informative
- **Conclusion**
    - Critical multiplism is about questioning data, understanding what can truly be answered, and challenging outcomes
    - Research involves proxies and approximations of knowledge, with correlation not equaling causation
    - The approach helps achieve validity by triangulating data points and considering multiple perspectives
### Unobtrusive Measures

Discussion about how unobtrusive measures can be used in research and everyday life.
- **Details**
    - **E.J.**: Shared an example of observing students' natural interactions without alerting them to being observed
    - **Daniel**: Related this to using proxies to understand if students comprehend material by watching their interactions
    - **Daniel**: Provided an everyday example of people choosing the fastest line at airports or grocery stores based on unobtrusive observations
- **Conclusion**
    - Unobtrusive measures can provide valuable data points without influencing subject behavior
    - These measures can be incorporated into critical multiplism approaches to strengthen research
