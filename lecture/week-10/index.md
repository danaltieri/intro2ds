## Overview

This module introduces correlation analysis as a tool for understanding relationships between variables. Students will calculate and interpret correlation coefficients and critically distinguish correlation from causation.

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this module, you should be able to:

1. **Calculate** correlation coefficients using Excel
1. **Interpret** the strength and direction of correlations
1. **Distinguish** between correlation and causation
1. **Apply** correlation analysis to your group project data

## Key Concepts

- Pearson correlation coefficient
- Positive and negative correlation
- Correlation vs. causation
- Scatterplots and correlation matrices
