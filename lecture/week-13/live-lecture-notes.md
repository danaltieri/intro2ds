## Discussed Topics

### Communicating Research Findings

- **Know your audience**: Tailor complexity and vocabulary to decision-makers, not statisticians
- **Storytelling with data**: Lead with the insight, support with evidence
- **Executive summaries**: What is the problem, what did you find, what does it mean?
- Visualization choices for non-technical audiences

### Structuring the Final Presentation

- 10-minute format: problem → data → method → findings → implications
- Slide design principles: simplicity, consistency, high signal-to-noise ratio
- Anticipating and preparing for audience questions
- Recording logistics: recordings due before final class session

### Research Ethics

- IRB and informed consent basics
- Data privacy: anonymization and secure storage
- Avoiding p-hacking and selective reporting
- Transparency about AI use in analysis
- Responsibilities when findings have organizational implications
