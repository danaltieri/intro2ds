## Overview

This module introduces linear regression as a tool for prediction and explanation. Students will build and interpret regression models, understand key assumptions, and apply regression to their group project data.

## Required Readings

- **Gutman and Goldmeier** - Chapter 8
- **Ozturk et al. 2022**
- **Merlino et al. 2022**
- **Namin et al. 2022**

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this module, you should be able to:

1. **Build** simple and multiple linear regression models in Excel
1. **Interpret** regression coefficients and R-squared values
1. **Assess** regression assumptions
1. **Apply** regression to predictive modeling in your group project

## Key Concepts

- Simple vs. multiple regression
- Regression coefficients and intercept
- R-squared and model fit
- Regression assumptions (linearity, independence, normality, homoscedasticity)
- Prediction vs. explanation
