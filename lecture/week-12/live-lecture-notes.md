## Discussed Topics

### Beyond Regression: Classification and Clustering

- **Classification models**: Predict categorical outcomes (e.g., churn vs. no churn)
- **Decision trees**: Interpretable models useful in organizational settings
- **Clustering**: Grouping observations by similarity without a predefined outcome
- Discussed trade-offs between model complexity and interpretability

### How AI and Machine Learning Work

- Overview of supervised vs. unsupervised learning
- How LLMs generate text: pattern matching at massive scale
- Wolfram's explanation of neural networks and emergent behavior
- Key insight: AI models are probabilistic, not deterministic

### AI-Assisted Data Analysis

- Using Claude, Gemini, and Microsoft Copilot for data exploration
- Prompting strategies for data analysis tasks
- Checking and verifying AI-generated outputs
- AI as a tool to augment, not replace, analytical judgment

### Ethical Considerations

- Bias in training data and its downstream effects
- Transparency and explainability of AI models
- Privacy concerns in organizational data use
- Responsible disclosure of AI use in research
