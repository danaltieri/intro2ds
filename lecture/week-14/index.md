## Learning Objectives

By the end of this module, you should be able to:

1. **Present** research findings professionally to peers and instructor
1. **Demonstrate** mastery of course concepts through applied analysis
1. **Provide** constructive peer feedback on data science projects
1. **Reflect** on personal growth and learning throughout the course
