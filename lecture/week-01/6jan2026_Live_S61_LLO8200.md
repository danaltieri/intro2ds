## Key takeaways

- Daniel Altieri introduced himself as the adjunct instructor for the Introduction to Data Science course, collaborating with Dr. Doyle
- The course will focus on data science fundamentals with a major group project component requiring 4-5 students per group
- AI use is permitted in the course with proper disclosure, contrary to the original syllabus statement
- Students should form groups and identify data sets for their term projects, with the first assignment due before the next class
- The final project will include both a group presentation and report, with presentations to be recorded before the final class

### Weekly assignments and reflections

Daniel reviewed the weekly assignments and reflection requirements.
- **Details**
    - **Daniel**: Weekly reflections are due before each live class
    - **Daniel**: Weekly assignments are updates on group projects (250 words minimum)
    - **Daniel**: Assignments build progressively: team formation, problem definition, variable identification, data exploration, etc.
- **Conclusion**
    - Two reflections can be dropped
    - Reflections should be posted as plain text, not documents
    - Assignments should be uploaded as PDF, doc, or plain text
### AI policy

Daniel discussed his approach to AI use in the course, which differs from the original syllabus.
- **Details**
    - **Daniel**: Original syllabus had a "no AI use" statement which he disagrees with
    - **Daniel**: Created an AI teaching assistant for the course
    - **Evan**: Shared that he works with AI in his role as director of academic technology
    - **Skye**: Expressed concern about student apathy when using AI for assignments
    - **Adrienne**: Mentioned her company is implementing "digital employees" and replacing junior staff with AI
- **Conclusion**
    - Students may use AI tools (ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Microsoft Copilot) but must disclose usage
    - Students remain responsible for fact-checking AI outputs
    - An AI use declaration must be included with assignments

### Final project presentation format

Daniel proposed a format for the final project presentations.
- **Details**
