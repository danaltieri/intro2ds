## Overview

This first module introduces fundamental research concepts and the role of statistics in research. You’ll develop statistical thinking skills and learn to recognize how data analysis supports research decisions.

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this module, you should be able to:

1. **Understand** fundamental research concepts and terminology
1. **Recognize** the role of statistics in research design and analysis
1. **Apply** basic statistical thinking to research questions
1. **Identify** appropriate statistical approaches for different research scenarios

## Key Concepts

- Research design fundamentals
- Role of statistics in research
- Types of research questions
- Introduction to data analysis
- Statistical reasoning
