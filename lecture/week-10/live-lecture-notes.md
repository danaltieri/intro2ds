## Discussed Topics

### What is Correlation?

- Measures the strength and direction of the linear relationship between two variables
- Ranges from -1 (perfect negative) to +1 (perfect positive)
- A coefficient near 0 indicates little or no linear relationship

### Interpreting Correlation Coefficients

- Strong: |r| ≥ 0.7
- Moderate: 0.4 ≤ |r| < 0.7
- Weak: |r| < 0.4
- Context matters: what counts as meaningful depends on the field

### Correlation vs. Causation

- Correlation does not imply causation
- Spurious correlations: classic examples discussed
- Need for experimental design or additional evidence to establish causation
